# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience for the public marketing website (frontend/src/pages/Website/*): riders (bikers/motorists) who have not yet installed the RideClub app, evaluating whether to download it. Secondary: press/partners looking to understand what RideClub is. The website's job is to convert first-time visitors into app installs — this is distinct from the app itself and the admin panel, which are out of scope for this surface.

## Product Purpose

RideClub is a group-ride safety and coordination app: live location sharing during rides, SOS/incident reporting, and group ride planning. The website exists to explain this and drive app downloads.

## Positioning

Group ride safety and live tracking is the core differentiator the website should lead with — real-time location sharing, SOS/incident reporting, and staying connected as a group while riding — ahead of route planning or community/social framing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing stack: React + TypeScript + Vite (frontend/), FastAPI backend, Supabase Postgres — confirmed by existing codebase, not re-asked.
- Scope: this product record and its associated design work apply to the public website pages only (frontend/src/pages/Website/**). The in-app UI (RidePlus, Navigation, MapView, etc.) and the Admin panel are explicitly out of scope and must not be touched by website design work.

## Brand Commitments

None binding. Full visual overhaul authorized — no fixed palette, typography, or accent-color commitment carried over from the existing app UI (which uses #FF6B22 orange); the website may choose its own direction.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, case studies, press mentions, or customer logos on hand. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

- Lead with safety: live tracking, SOS, and incident reporting are the headline story, not a feature buried in a list.
- Every page's job is to move a first-time visitor toward installing the app — clarity and a strong CTA outrank decorative flourish.
- Speak to riders, not enterprises: tone and imagery should read as rider-community, not corporate SaaS.
- Keep app/admin surfaces untouched — this product record and any resulting DESIGN.md govern the public website only.
